How Taskora works

  1. Pick the box. Is it urgent (24–48 hours)? Is it important (moves your goals)? The box answers both.
  2. Type and press Enter. Each box has its own input at the bottom.
  3. Notes. Open Notes from the pencil in the top-left corner. Type / to pick a tag, write your task after it, then press Schedule.
  4. Shortcuts. / new task, Space complete, F focus, Esc cancel.
